Image sourcing guide
Where to get K-drama & Kpop fashion photos legally
Licensed & safe sources
1. Official press kits (FREE)
Email press offices directly: Netflix Korea (press.kr@netflix.com), tvN (tvn_pr@cjenm.com), JTBC. Request episode stills and character wardrobe shots. Usually free for editorial/media use with credit. Best quality.
2. InkiStyle.com — editorial partnership
inkistyle.com documents every K-drama outfit per episode. Contact them for image sharing agreement. They are a small team and often open to cross-promotion with credits.
→ inkistyle.com
3. Getty Images editorial license (~$200/mo)
Covers red carpet, concert, and press tour photos for media reporting. Search "[idol name] [event] 2026" — gives legitimate photos from MAMA, Melon Music Awards, press tours.
→ gettyimages.com
4. Unsplash editorial (FREE — current placeholder source)
Current placeholder. Good for generic Korean street style photos. Not specific enough for drama character looks — replace with press kit images when available.
→ unsplash.com
5. AI-generated illustrations (FREE, original IP)
Use Midjourney or Stable Diffusion: prompt "Korean fashion editorial illustration, [style name], no face visible, flat lay style, cream and [color] tones" — legally your own IP, copyright-free, consistent visual style.
