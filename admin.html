Photo sourcing guide
How to get real K-drama and K-pop fashion photos legally
⚠️ Current problem: Unsplash photos are generic Western editorial — not Korean
The card photos currently show random Western fashion editorial images from Unsplash. They have nothing to do with K-dramas or K-pop. There is no free API that automatically serves matching Korean fashion photos. This requires manual curation — but here is the fastest path to fix it.
Best sources in order of ease
1. InkiStyle.com — K-drama outfit photos (BEST, FREE)
InkiStyle documents every K-drama outfit with episode stills, character names, and brand info. Email them at contact@inkistyle.com for an editorial image-sharing agreement. Small team, usually open to cross-promotion. Best quality for drama-specific photos.
→ inkistyle.com
2. Official press kits (FREE)
Email press offices directly: Netflix Korea press — pressroom.netflix.com/ko · MBC PR — tvcast.naver.com/mbc · tvN — search "tvN press kit [drama name]". Request episode stills. Usually free for editorial use with credit.
3. Pinterest boards (Free, manually curated)
Search "[Drama name] fashion outfit" or "[Idol name] airport fashion 2026" on Pinterest. Fan boards are extensive and episode-specific. Use the photo URL directly in admin if it's a press or promotional image — not paparazzi shots.
→ pinterest.com
4. Getty Images editorial license (~$200/mo)
Covers red carpet, concert, press tour photos for media reporting. Search "[idol] [event] 2026". The cleanest paid option. Use the embed code Getty provides — it includes attribution automatically.
→ gettyimages.com
5. Midjourney / Stable Diffusion (FREE, original IP)
Prompt: "Korean fashion editorial illustration, [style name] aesthetic, no face visible, flat lay style, cream and [palette] tones, Vogue Korea editorial". Legally your own IP. Consistent visual language. Good stopgap while sourcing real photos.
Test a Google Image Search keyword
Formula: [Idol name] [group] [event] [year] outfit fashion — be very specific
